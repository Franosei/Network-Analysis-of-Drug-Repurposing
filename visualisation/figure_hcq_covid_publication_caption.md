**Figure 1 | Evidence funnel for the hydroxychloroquine-COVID-19 literature.** The retrieval returned 3,391 records. Exact drug-disease verification retained 3,108 HCQ-COVID-19 records and excluded 283 records that did not contain the verified pair. Semantic classification then identified 893 therapeutic records, 1,271 adverse or conflicting records and 944 irrelevant records. Thus, 28.7% of the classified exact-pair records were therapeutic. The funnel makes the data-quality sequence explicit: retrieval volume, pair verification and semantic classification are separate operations. Retrieval volume is not treated as therapeutic support.

**Source and run note:** Values are from `outputs/20260923_hcq_covid_full_pipeline`, generated on 23 September 2026. The trial count is the canonical ledger count after full-registry mapping, while the literature count is the exact-pair verified set. The target edge was withheld during graph evaluation.

**Figure 2 | Publication volume and evidence dependence.** Schematic. Twelve publications drawing on five underlying studies or data sources. Arrows indicate the direction underlying source → derived publication. Counting publications treats them as twelve independent pieces of evidence; a lineage-aware count recognises five. Pooled analyses (P3, P8) inherit their sources' evidence rather than adding to it.

**Abbreviations:** CrI, credible interval; FAERS, FDA Adverse Event Reporting System; HCQ, hydroxychloroquine; PMC, PubMed Central.
