**Figure 1 | From heterogeneous records to an integrated evidence ledger: the hydroxychloroquine-COVID-19 case.** The full ClinicalTrials.gov registry snapshot yielded 94 canonical trial records for the pair. An uncapped PubMed/PMC retrieval returned 3,391 records, of which 3,108 passed exact drug and disease verification and were classified as benefit (893), null, harm or conflicting (1,271), or irrelevant (944). FAERS contributed a safety-overlap coefficient of gamma = 0.75 across eight terms. In a leakage-controlled graph test, 2,267 alternative length-3 paths were available and the graph probability was 1.000. The Bayesian ledger therefore reports an Evidence Posterior Index of 0.2024 (95% pseudo-Beta CrI 0.155 to 0.254) and an evidence-readiness score of 74.9/100. Readiness summarises coverage, consistency and auditability; it is not a probability of efficacy. Colours identify evidence domains rather than evidence strength.

**Source and run note:** Values are from `outputs/20260923_hcq_covid_full_pipeline`, generated on 23 September 2026.
